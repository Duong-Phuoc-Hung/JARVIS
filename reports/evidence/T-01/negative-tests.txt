SCOPED SECURITY / TRUTHFULNESS REGRESSION (all PASS within 301-case suite)
- HTTP read-only fallback rejects click/type/wait/scroll/screenshot and reports
  driver=http_scraper; unsupported localStorage cannot poison an otherwise valid HTTP read.
- Driver detection requires successful launch and verified close; arbitrary HTTP 200 at a CDP
  endpoint is rejected, failed candidates are closed, and launch-time CDP config is refreshed.
- Concurrent action/result reads are serialized; a result cannot inherit another request's URL.
- Playwright sync handles stay on one owner thread, including core handlers invoked elsewhere.
- Empty workflow, partial form fill, failed submit, stale scrape, and empty/error-title documents
  cannot normalize to SUCCESS.
- Scroll distance is validated before JavaScript evaluation.
- Download requests receive only cookies applicable to the destination domain/path/scheme/expiry;
  host-only cookies do not reach subdomains, and cookies are recomputed on every redirect hop.
- Custom headers are granted only to the exact authorized origin/request frame, are stripped on
  cross-origin redirects, cannot be spent by a hostile iframe, and are never restored after a
  cross-origin bounce.
- Cookie validation is fail-closed across PSL public suffixes, UTS46 IDNA, IPv4/IPv6, default
  paths, trailing dots, browser expiry limits and invalid numeric values. Ordered duplicate
  cookie headers and Set-Cookie values retain browser-observed order.
- CHIPS partition keys and cross-site-ancestor variants survive capture/save/restore; targeted
  expiry/deletion cannot remove the sibling variant, and Secure is mandatory for partitioned
  cookies.
- localStorage is applied only to an exact matching origin and not after cross-origin redirect.
- Session JSON writes use a unique temp file, retry atomic replace on Windows, and clean failures.
- Price extraction rejects zero/non-finite/overflow/unassociated offers, does not invent
  stock/shipping, and leaves the product URL empty when no link was observed.
- Browser, session, factory, core, and legacy errors/logs do not echo selectors, form values,
  exception secrets, query credentials, cookies, tokens, failed page titles, or stale content.
- Explicit legacy user_data_dir state persists through the canonical session seam.
- Browser health reports READY/LIMITED/ERROR truthfully and returns 0/2/1 respectively; it never
  prints an unconditional all-checks-passed summary.

No credential was needed for this loopback-only real-browser test. No external live service was
contacted. Mock cases remain unit evidence only and were not counted among the 21 E2E cases.
